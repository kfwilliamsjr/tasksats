# TaskSats Phase 1

## Goal

Launch a focused front-end and product direction for TaskSats as a Bitcoin payment product for service businesses.

## Core Promise

TaskSats helps service businesses create invoices, accept Bitcoin and Lightning payments, and confirm payments through a simple, reliable customer experience.

## Recommended Domain Roles

- `tasksats.com`
  Use as the primary production domain for the main business website and product.
- `tasksats.ai`
  Use as a secondary domain for experiments, AI-focused campaigns, future automations, or redirects.

## Phase 1 Scope

- Public marketing site
- Clear product positioning
- Dual USD / BTC price presentation
- Hosted invoice and payment flow preview
- Clear launch offers
- Basic domain strategy on-site

## Backend for Next Phase

- Merchant accounts
- Invoice creation
- Hosted checkout generation
- Payment confirmation via webhook
- Email notifications
- Dashboard and payment history

## Recommended Build Sequence

1. Convert the current front-end direction into a production web app shell.
2. Implement invoice creation and hosted checkout.
3. Add payment confirmation and merchant notifications.
4. Add dashboard and reporting.

## Notes

- Keep the first release narrow.
- Prioritize reliability, trust, and clarity over feature count.
- Grow into broader commerce or marketplace features only after payment usage is proven.
